Our goal

To make recycling on campus easy and rewarding, so recyclables get sorted correctly, bins get emptied before they overflow, and janitors no longer have to sort waste by hand.

Tap/Scan, drop, earn

Sortify takes the guesswork out of recycling. You don't have to work out which bin is right, because the bin sorts every item for you.

1

Tap/Scan

Tap your university ID on the card reader at the front of the bin. Or scan the QR code with your phone, type in your university ID and press Start recycling.

2

Drop

Drop one item in the top. The conveyor carries it and a pusher sends it to the metal, paper or plastic bin.

3

Earn

Every item earns you 5 points. Every 100 points gets you a 10% discount at local cafés and restaurants.
